🐾 Dividing Polynomials Using Synthetic Division

Algebra II • Interactive Lesson • I Do / We Do / You Do

🎯 Mastery goal: 80% or higher

1. 📘 Lesson Guide / Overview

Learning Target: I can divide a polynomial by a linear binomial of the form x − c using synthetic division, interpret the quotient and remainder, and check my answer.

Why it matters: Synthetic division provides an efficient way to divide polynomials and helps us evaluate polynomial functions and identify factors and zeros.

Success Criteria: I can identify the correct synthetic number, include missing coefficients as zeros, bring down, multiply, add, and write the quotient and remainder accurately.

MAGIC / mastery focus: Model a method, actively respond, get immediate feedback, and demonstrate independent mastery.

Whole Group • 5 minutes

2. 🧰 Materials & Resources

Student device, this interactive lesson, notebook or scratch paper, and Canvas. Optional: teacher screen sharing, mini-whiteboards, and a calculator for checking arithmetic.

Vocabulary: dividend (polynomial being divided), divisor (expression you divide by), coefficient (number multiplying a variable), quotient (result), remainder (leftover), synthetic number (zero of the linear divisor).

Whole Group

3. 🔔 Warm-Up / Bell Ringer

Review integer operations and identifying coefficients. Enter your answers and check them.

Whole Group • 5 minutes

4. 👩‍🏫 I Do – Direct Instruction (Learn)

Teacher model: (x² − 3x − 10) ÷ (x − 5). The synthetic number is 5 (not −5). Write the coefficients 1, −3, −10.

  1. Set up: Put 5 on the left and coefficients across the top.
  2. Bring down: Move 1 to the bottom row.
  3. Multiply: 5 × 1 = 5; place 5 under −3.
  4. Add: −3 + 5 = 2.
  5. Multiply: 5 × 2 = 10; place 10 under −10.
  6. Add: −10 + 10 = 0 (remainder).
  7. Interpret: Quotient is x + 2; remainder 0.

Check: (x − 5)(x + 2) + 0 = x² − 3x − 10. Remember: when a power is missing, insert a 0 coefficient.

Whole Group • 10 minutes

5. 🤝 We Do – Guided Practice

Complete every box. The top row contains the synthetic number and coefficients. The middle row contains multiplication results. The bottom row contains the brought-down coefficient, sums, and remainder. Then enter the quotient. Each problem uses a different color.

Whole Group • 12 minutes

6. ✍️ You Do – Independent Practice

Try these on your own. Use Check Work after completing the entire setup. The quotient must be written in simplified form (for example, x+2).

Independent • 12 minutes

7. 🌱 Differentiation / Accommodations

Small group reteach: Use the same 4-step chant: Bring down → Multiply → Add → Repeat. Color-code each column, model one calculation at a time, and provide a coefficient organizer. Start with a divisor x − c and no missing terms before introducing zero placeholders.

Support: Allow extra time, chunk steps, read directions aloud, provide a multiplication chart when appropriate, and give students the synthetic number or coefficients as scaffolds.

Extension: Divide a cubic with a missing term, interpret a nonzero remainder, and verify using multiplication or the Remainder Theorem.

Small Group / Individual
